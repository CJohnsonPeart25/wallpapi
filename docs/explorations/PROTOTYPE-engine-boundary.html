Where does Score end and Zone begin?

The engine (taste) ranks Items; wallpapi turns that into Zones (Banger, Unknown, Dud) and fills a Batch by its Mix. The question: what exactly crosses the seam? Three structures: A the engine hands back a Score only (today's shape: Unknown means "Score is exactly zero"); B a Score plus how much evidence lies near the Item (its support) and a veto flag, and wallpapi draws the Zone lines itself; C the engine hands back its own three-way reading and wallpapi maps it 1:1. Place Judgements on the map, switch structure and scoring method, and watch the Zones and the Batch move. Toy space: the map is a 2D picture of the embedding space; nearness on it is similarity.

Click the map to record a Judgement of the chosen kind. Click a candidate dot to see what crossed the seam for it. ★ Favourite · ● Like · ✕ Ban · ▪ Ignore. Ringed dots are in the Batch.
